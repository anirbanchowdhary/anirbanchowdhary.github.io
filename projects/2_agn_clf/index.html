Conditional Luminosity Functions of AGN

Joint luminosity-function and clustering constraints on the AGN–halo connection

Baryon acoustic oscillation peaks in the clustering of DESI galaxies, quasars (QSO) and the Lyman-alpha forest across 11 billion years of cosmic history: two-point statistics of tracers at work. Credit: DESI Collaboration/A. de Mattia. Cropped from the original on Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0.

I model the AGN conditional luminosity function (CLF), which describes how AGN luminosity depends on host halo mass, in the TNG300 simulation for 0.1 < z < 3.

I developed a joint MCMC framework that fits the luminosity function and the two-point correlation function together to recover the host halos of quasars.

Paper: Chowdhary, Chatterjee & Das (2026), Extracting Host Dark Matter Halos of Quasars at Intermediate Redshifts: A Combined Clustering and Luminosity Function Based Approach, submitted to MNRAS.

Presented at: Cosmic Connections Second Edition: AI/ML Across Cosmic Scales (IMSc Chennai, September 2026).
