Summary Theoretical and computational astrophysicist working at the interface of cosmology, galaxy formation and evolution. I study the co-evolution of supermassive black holes, galaxies and dark matter halos with cosmological hydrodynamic simulations, and develop statistical measures that connect simulation predictions to galaxy-survey observations.

Work

  • 2022 - Present
    PhD Research Scholar, School of Astrophysics
    Presidency University, Kolkata
    Advisor: Dr. Suchetana Chatterjee. Thesis (tentative): Large Scale Structures in the High Redshift Universe.
    • Studying the co-evolution of supermassive black holes and dark matter halos in cosmological hydrodynamic simulations (IllustrisTNG, SIMBA).
    • Quantifying the AGN–halo connection with halo occupation distribution (HOD) and conditional luminosity function (CLF) frameworks.
    • Developing a joint statistical framework combining luminosity functions and two-point correlation functions to constrain the AGN–halo connection and reconstruct quasar host halo properties.
