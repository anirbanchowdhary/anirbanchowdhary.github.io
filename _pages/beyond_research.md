### How I got here

I grew up in a small town without much exposure to the world of science. What I did have was curiosity: I was always an inquisitive child. It was my professors in college who turned that curiosity into a path towards astrophysics.

### Mountains and hitchhiking

I grew up at the foothills of the Himalaya, and I love the mountains. I usually take short trips, mostly hitchhiking, to the villages around, to connect with the people and their culture. My first hitchhike, to Sandakphu, has been the most memorable.

### Food in Kolkata

Kolkata has become a new home and my favourite food destination. If you are visiting, this is where I would point you:

- **Apanjan** for fish fry
- **Kafulok** for Indo-Chinese
- **Sienna Cafe** for modern Bengali fusion
- **Kasturi** for Bengali cuisine
