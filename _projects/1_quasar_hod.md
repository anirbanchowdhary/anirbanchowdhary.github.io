I model the Halo Occupation Distribution (HOD) of quasars using the cosmological hydrodynamic simulations [IllustrisTNG](https://www.tng-project.org/) and [SIMBA](http://simba.roe.ac.uk/), studying how occupation depends on luminosity, redshift, black hole mass and AGN feedback mode.

Both simulations show quasar activity being quenched at a characteristic halo mass of roughly 10<sup>13</sup> M<sub>☉</sub>. The goal is to build testable mock observables for next-generation galaxy surveys that can break degeneracies between simulation models.

**Paper:** Chowdhary & Chatterjee (2025), [ApJ 992, 21](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/adfb6d) · [arXiv:2508.08851](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08851)
